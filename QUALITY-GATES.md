# Quality gates — v6.8.0

| Gate | Must demonstrate | Blocks when |
|---|---|---|
| G0 Definition | objective, audience, action, landing scope and material inventory | a choice would materially change the project and remains unknown |
| G1 Strategy & Content | compiled context, current reference evidence, challenged flow, Experience Spine, exact thesis/CTA lock, truthful claims and scene map | context, causal progression, content lock, structural specificity or claim evidence is missing |
| G2 Creative Direction | project-specific premium bar, three physically distant territories, isolated selection, one generated master, confirmation and complete design genome | territories converge, selection is self-approved, source traceability fails or the master/genome is incomplete |
| G3 Visual Experience | scene grammar, direct/high-end comparison for every section, derived foundations, explicit colors, responsive evidence, coherent rhythm and conditional spatial-medium comparison | any scene lacks relational grammar, value decision, color assignment, responsive behavior or master continuity; spatial spectacle lacks physical advantage over a simpler medium |
| G4 Production | structural build, render-derived media decisions, returned assets, conditional spatial spike/storyboard/state QA, integrated landing, per-scene desktop/mobile traversal, content parity, integrity manifest and independent review | runtime evidence is incomplete/reused/stale, a selected spatial state or fallback is unproved, locked copy drifts, the build changes after review or critical quality is missing |
| G5 Release | G0–G4 approved, fresh SHA-256-linked evidence, physical delivery checks, no blocking findings and a reusable fingerprint | state, evidence, implementation, content or recorded direction disagree |

Scripts decide whether observable evidence exists and is internally consistent. Agent 07 decides whether rendered design quality is sufficient.
