## Scope

Landing creation only. Triggered by a real hero that satisfied nominal media/effect checks while remaining visually flat and interchangeable.

## KEEP

- freedom to choose one or several typefaces;
- static, restrained and editorial directions as valid outcomes;
- existing eight roles, six gates, twelve stages and twelve project artifacts;
- effect selection by meaning and fit rather than spectacle.

## IMPROVE

- validate future stress-test evidence against real desktop/mobile captures and interaction recordings;
- learn from additional completed heroes before adding any further aesthetic pattern;
- avoid turning the six axes into numeric taste scoring.

## ADD

- six-axis `Hero experience stress test` inside the existing visual artifact;
- palette interchangeability and major-element removal countertests;
- explicit rejection of adjacent media and nominal/generic effects as defining mechanisms;
- physical `source/file#marker` proof for `FX-* FINAL`;
- regression tests for unresolved hero axes and absent effect implementation.

## REMOVE

- ingredient-count approval of a hero;
- treating common aesthetic clusters as direction without subject provenance;
- treating a shadow, fade or planned transition as delivered mechanism;
- the assumption that “premium” means automatically adding motion, 3D or another font.
