# v2.1 → v2.2 · Creative Direction & Production Audit

**Date:** 2026-08-19  
**Result:** PASS  
**Scope:** direction selection, creative technology, media/assets, downstream ownership, machine-readable pipeline, project templates and fail-closed validation.

## Root problem

v2.1 was strong at controlling execution **after a direction had been chosen**. It did not require enough evidence to prove that the chosen direction was the best viable direction for the project.

The failure mode was therefore subtle:

> a coherent, generic or weak direction could pass through a very rigorous downstream process and become a polished wrong answer.

## Perspective A — Creative direction selection

### Finding A1 — Art Direction could converge too early — HIGH

04 considered territories but owned the dominant direction before a mandatory comparative visual/experiential validation step.

**Fix v2.2:** 04 now explores multiple experience directions; the minimum comes from the delivery profile. G5 enters `REVIEW`, then DRS/CT01/M01 produce independent evidence and V00 validates comparatively before 00 can approve G5.

### Finding A2 — Alternatives could be cosmetic — HIGH

The system did not machine/process-enforce the distinction between different concepts and skins of the same layout.

**Fix:** direction exploration explicitly compares composition, typography, media system, rhythm, navigation and interaction. V00 rejects alternatives that are not materially divergent.

### Finding A3 — Validation medium was too tool-shaped — MEDIUM

The repo documented Figma + Browser well, but that pair could be misread as the canonical workflow.

**Fix:** evidence-first validation. Image/SVG/Figma/storyboard/video/browser/prototype are all valid when they prove the right hypothesis. Interaction-defined concepts require behavioral evidence; static concepts do not need unnecessary runtime.

## Perspective B — Creative production / technical ambition

### Finding B1 — No owner for creative mechanisms — HIGH

Art/Motion/Frontend jointly touched creative JS, scroll, WebGL/3D/canvas and prototypes, but nobody owned the question “what technical mechanism best serves this experience?”

**Fix:** `CT01 · Creative Technology` owns mechanism selection/advisory, risk prototypes and fallbacks. It does not own art, motion timing or production architecture.

### Finding B2 — Media was an optional extension, but media decisions are core to many modern websites — HIGH

Image/video/3D/generated assets affect direction selection, responsive, performance and final QA even when no specialist pipeline is installed.

**Fix:** `M01 · Media & Asset Production` becomes core for strategy/readiness/finalization. Specialized virtual-production/3D/DAM pipelines can still be extensions.

### Finding B3 — Specific tools risk becoming architecture — MEDIUM

Figma, Higgsfield, GSAP, Three.js, Blender, Spline and similar tools can be valuable but are not universally available or appropriate.

**Fix:** provider/tool-neutral contracts. The system selects medium/mechanism first, then uses any suitable available provider. No paid tool is required by core.

### Finding B4 — Final Visual QA could baseline provisional media — HIGH

A layout could be visually reviewed while an essential image/video/3D asset was still only a misleading placeholder.

**Fix:** `media-finalization` runs after Functional QA and before G12; it is also a G14 release prerequisite.

## Perspective C — Governance / engineering

### Finding C1 — New creative promises must be fail-closed — CRITICAL

Adding agents without validator semantics would allow a future edit to delete Direction Validation from G5 while docs still claimed it was mandatory.

**Fix:** `MANDATORY_CORE_CHECKPOINTS` in `tools/validate_system.py` guards G5/G6/G7/G9/G10/G11/G12/G14 core checkpoints.

### Finding C2 — Delivery profile needed explicit exploration depth — HIGH

**Fix:** `config/profiles.json` now declares `direction_exploration_minimum` and `direction_evidence_depth`; validator rejects minimums below 2 and requires monotonic lean ≤ standard ≤ critical.

### Finding C3 — Mutation tests needed to prove the guards — HIGH

**Fix:** two new tests deliberately remove V00 from G5 and reduce direction exploration below 2. Both fail validation as intended. The suite contains 14 tests.
