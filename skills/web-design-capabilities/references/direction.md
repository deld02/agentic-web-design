# Direction capability

## Primary: subject-grounded distinctiveness

Before composing, state the concrete subject, audience and single job of the page. Derive visual material from the subject's world: language, artifacts, materiality, processes, place and culture. Commit to a specific point of view across typography, palette, composition, imagery and rhythm.

In G2, synthesize the research into one artistic master rather than competing webpage layouts. Compare that master against the current reference evidence for specificity and grounding. In G3, compare a restrained direct form with a master-led challenger for each primary scene. Never infer style from a sector label or assume the more expressive form wins.

For the master define:

- one-sentence thesis;
- the hero as the clearest expression of that thesis;
- one memorable device grounded in the project;
- one justified aesthetic risk;
- rules and anti-rules;
- why the direction could not be exchanged with a nearby competitor.
- a media thesis and at least one representative, project-specific visual sample that makes the direction judgeable.
- one `FX-*` mechanism with visible static, simple and expressive evidence; evaluate `DEPTH_3D` when subject matter has volume, material, space, transformation or layered perspective.

When G3 translates the master, difference between baseline and challenger must be structural or conceptual, not a palette/font skin. For the hero, test integration rather than ingredient count: copy, type, color, media and the mechanism must alter one another's reading. A visual placed beside copy, a common palette cluster or a generic transition does not become distinctive by being documented. Compare the signature palette with a plausible neutral/category alternative and remove one major element; if the hero's logic does not change, revise the relationship. This does not mandate multiple typefaces, 3D or motion.

Use research before composing. If visual evidence disproves a research decision, return the finding to its owner; do not silently replace it with a preferred palette, typeface, layout or mechanism.

If the user says “make your proposal” or “choose for me”, 03 completes the master and records delegation without changing the brief or skipping review. Delegation does not let this capability approve G2.

The artistic master is always a substantial project-specific generated image. Large type, icons, gradients, generic CSS patterns, placeholders and webpage screenshots do not satisfy it. Documentary subjects still require authentic supplied media; the master may use honest conceptual, material or atmospheric representation.

Mechanism and motion intensity are explored during G3 scene translation, using `docs/methods/resource-selection.md` when needed. A component demo is a candidate to translate, never an identity to paste.

## Automatic taste challenge

Run this once on the G2 master before confirmation and again on its G3 translation when needed. It challenges the work but cannot approve a gate or replace 07.

Treat these as suspects, not universal bans: purple/blue hero gradients, default geometric sans typography, centered hero-plus-pill CTA, three repeated icon cards, soft white shadow cards, decorative blobs/glows/grain and motion copied from a component demo. A suspect passes only when project or brand evidence makes it specific and removing it weakens the visual thesis.

Ask:

- could the same composition, palette and type be relabeled for a nearby competitor?
- is the most memorable element derived from the subject or from a common UI pattern?
- do type, media, color and mechanism change one another, or merely sit together?
- does the direction survive removal of its most fashionable treatment?

Return `KEEP`, `ITERATE` or `KILL` with reference to the physical evidence. Allow at most one targeted correction. Do not prescribe a font family, saturated accent, fixed spacing grid, card count, 3D or motion intensity.

## Logging

In `creative-direction.md`, record capability ID, mode, reason, adopted/rejected principles and resulting candidate. The challenger never selects the winner.
